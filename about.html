Our Brand Story

About Oud & Aura

“

At Oud & Aura, we believe a fragrance should do more than smell good — it should leave an impression.

”

We created Oud & Aura with one simple idea: premium fragrance shouldn't require a premium price tag.

With high-concentration perfumes, carefully selected fragrances and a collection that moves from rich oriental scents to fresh, modern compositions, we create fragrances made to be enjoyed without constant reapplication.

Every fragrance is chosen and tested with one question in mind: does it live up to the experience we promise?

From everyday wear to special occasions and gifting, Oud & Aura is about lasting fragrance, effortless elegance, and a scent you'll want to wear again.

The Way We Create

Our Approach

We focus on what truly matters in a fragrance — quality, concentration, variety and lasting presence.

opacity

High Concentration

More fragrance. Less compromise.

Our perfumes are made with up to 50% fragrance oil concentration, giving them the depth and staying power expected from a premium fragrance.

schedule

Made to Last

Wear it. Forget the reapply.

We test our fragrances for real-world wear because a beautiful scent means little if it disappears too soon. Our fragrances are designed for long-lasting performance, with many lasting 48+ hours depending on skin, fabric and conditions.

mood

A Scent for Every Mood

From oud to fresh, from bold to effortless.

We don't believe fragrance should fit into one category. Our collection explores oriental, woody, sweet, fresh and modern profiles, giving you something for every mood, occasion and personality.
